# Prepare a tender response

You work in sales for Nordlicht Fluidtechnik AG (USt-IdNr. DE811000777). Prepare one net EUR bid for the Hafenbetriebe
Elbstadt's procurement AZ-2027-0815, "Hydraulikzylinder für die Schleusentore Süd". The scenario date is 2026-11-10T09:00:00Z; the
submission deadline is 2026-12-11T12:00:00Z.

Four German websites hold what you need: the buyer's procurement platform (Beschaffungsplattform, `beschaffung`) with
the procedure and its Leistungsverzeichnis, the buyer's supplier portal (Lieferantenportal, `lieferantenportal`) with
the Leistungsbeschreibung and any Änderungsmitteilungen, your company's Warenwirtschaft (`warenwirtschaft`) with the
articles, stock, prices and the discount approvals (Freigaben), and the sales mailbox (Postfach, `postfach`).

## Rules

1. Quantity: use the Leistungsverzeichnis quantity, unless an Änderungsmitteilung for exactly this procedure changes it.
2. Material: each position names a requirement of the Leistungsbeschreibung; only its current (gültig) revision
   counts, not superseded ones and not drafts. Choose the active article whose bore, stroke, mounting and
   certification match that requirement exactly. Blocked (gesperrt) articles do not qualify.
3. Unit price: take the article's price scale with the highest minimum quantity no greater than the quantity, apply
   the approved discount and round the unit price half up to the cent.
4. Discount: the latest approval by the sales management for exactly this procedure, as recorded in the
   Warenwirtschaft's Freigaben. Approvals for other procedures and anything not recorded there do not count.
5. Lead time: the delivery-from-stock weeks when the freely available stock covers the quantity, otherwise the
   production weeks.
6. Total: the sum of quantity times unit price, rounded to the cent.

## Submission

On the procurement platform, open the procedure, choose "Teilnahme bekunden", then "Angebotsabgabe". Register with the
company name Nordlicht Fluidtechnik AG, the USt-IdNr. DE811000777 and a contact e-mail, choose Hauptangebot, configure
each position's article and enter the quantity, the discounted net unit price (German format, 1.234,56) and the lead
time. Enter the discount and the net total, acknowledge the tender documents, request a signing TAN, read it in the
Postfach, enter it and confirm "Angebot verbindlich abgeben" in the browser dialog. One submission ends the run.
