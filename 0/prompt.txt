The final hours changed everything.

At the TUM.ai EHL Grand Finale, we built an agentic RFQ-to-Bid system that could navigate unfamiliar enterprise websites, find the right documents, extract evidence, calculate a commercial bid, and submit it end to end.

What made it robust:
\- LLM-directed browsing instead of hard-coded website flows
\- Evidence-first extraction, source tracking, and coverage checks
\- Exact Decimal-based commercial calculations
\- Four parallel browsers, action verification, recovery paths, and pre-submit review gates

The biggest lesson: correctness and speed are separate challenges. Building reliable agents is not about one perfect prompt, it is about validation, recovery, structured evidence, and relentless iteration.
