QuoteCraft is an agentic solution for Atira’s “RFQ to Bid” challenge:

- Four model-directed browser agents investigate the tender portal, supplier portal, ERP, and mail in parallel.
- Relevant pages become typed evidence in a shared case file.
- A deterministic `Decimal` calculator applies the commercial rules.
- A final agent completes the bid form, retrieves the TAN from mail, and submits.
